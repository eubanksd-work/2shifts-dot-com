Where your data lives

Firms ask this before anything else, and they should. If any of the below doesn't meet your own policy requirements, we can write the difference into the scope.

Work happens in your accounts

Every automation built runs inside your own Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, on your tenant, under your licenses. Nothing is hosted elsewhere. There is no Second Shift server, no shared database, and no copy of your records stored outside your legal boundaries. When the pilot ends you aren't migrating off anything, because nothing was ever anywhere else.

No copies

No exports for testing, no screenshots of real records, no notes with client names in them. When sample data is needed for building, it is either your own data inside your own environment, or it is invented.

Access ends at handoff

You grant access when the engagement starts and you revoke it when the work is handed over. If you need support later, you grant access again for that piece of work, and it ends the same way.

Nothing is kept

After handoff no client data is held anywhere else. Not the records, not a configuration export, not a backup kept just in case. What you keep is the working automation in your own environment and a short written guide to it.

Nothing else is in the loop

The automations use the platforms you already pay for. Whatever Microsoft or Google does with data inside your tenant is your existing arrangement with them, not something added on top of it.

Your data is not routed through any other service. If a piece of work would genuinely be better with something else in the loop, it gets named, along with exactly what it would see, and your agreement in writing before it is used. The default is no, and it holds until you change it.

Email and booking

Correspondence between us sits in Google Workspace, the same as any business email. Please don't send client records that way. If something needs to be seen, we look at it in your environment.

The booking links on this site go to cal.com, which takes your name and email in order to put the call on a calendar. It sees nothing beyond what you type into it, and it is the only outside service involved before we've spoken.

This site

No analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts, and no fonts loaded from anyone else's servers. Nothing here records that you visited. There is no cookie banner because there are no cookies.

On paper

Most firms have an NDA they prefer, and using yours is simplest. If you'd rather not paper it separately, the pilot scope document carries a confidentiality clause, so something is always in place before work starts.

About AI

Yes, AI is part of how this work gets done. That is the point of it: the tedious reading, sorting and drafting a firm pays people to do by hand is exactly what these tools are good at.

It stays inside the same walls as everything else on this page. The AI features used are the ones already built into the Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace you pay for, running on your tenant, under your licenses, on your data, governed by the agreement you already have with that vendor. Nothing is pasted into a consumer chatbot, a personal account, or any service you have not agreed to.

If a piece of work would genuinely be better with an outside AI tool in the loop, the same rule applies as to any other service: it gets named, with exactly what it would see, and your agreement in writing before it is used.
